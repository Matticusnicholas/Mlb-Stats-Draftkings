Best Ball Metrics Explained:

  • BB Score: Composite 0-100 metric combining best week, consistency, and TEAR ability
  • Useful Pts: Total points from starting-worthy weeks (70th %ile threshold)
  • Useful%: Percentage of weeks that scored above starting threshold
  • Best Week: Highest 7-day rolling window points total
  • Boom%: Percentage of weeks scoring 90th percentile or higher
  • TEAR3/TEAR4: Multi-game hot streak rates (3+ or 4+ consecutive elite games)
