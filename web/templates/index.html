Best Ball Metrics Explained:

  • BB Score: Composite 0-100 metric combining best week, consistency, and TEAR ability
  • Useful Pts: Total points from starting-worthy weeks (70th %ile threshold)
  • Useful Wks: Count of weeks above starting threshold
  • Pts/Wk: Average points per useful week (concentration metric - higher = more explosive)
  • IV (Implied Volatility): Weekly explosiveness vs league average (1.0 = normal, 1.35+ = High-IV, 1.60+ = Gamma, 2.0+ = Nuclear)
  • Tier: IV classification - Nuclear (2.0+) > Gamma (1.60+) > High-IV (1.35+) > Normal (1.0) > Low-Vol (<1.0)
  • Best Week: Highest 7-day rolling window points total
  • Boom%: Percentage of weeks scoring 90th percentile or higher
  • TEAR3/TEAR4: Multi-game hot streaks (3+ or 4+ consecutive elite games)
  • Workhorse Score: Innings-eating reliability (0-100): durability, IP/start, quality starts
  • QS Rate: Percentage of starts with 6+ IP (quality starts)
